

*Dec 20th, 2025*

Well we broke up. That fear that I was wasting time or money in the relationship isn’t there anymore though because I do think it was still time well spent. I learned much about myself. I grew, emotionally and physically, and I’ll continue to see the effect she had on me every day when I look at the mirror. Yet I decided to break up with her.  

I think it boils down to: I felt as though she wasn’t showing up for me in ways I thought were necessary and important, and when confronted with that, or some version of that, there was acknowledgment but no signal that she was going to change soon. She said she instead was sacrificing being with her friends and experiencing being by herself in the city, to which I said I didn’t want to get in the way of either or didn’t want her to feel like she was giving something up to be with me. I asked if that could change, and she said she didn’t think it would be soon. I didn’t think it made sense to stay together if nothing changed. Cue the tears.


That confused me. Why cry but also not try to change? 


But I started crying too. And we were just there crying, and told each other we still loved each other. And suddenly those movie moments when there’s a breakup on screen and I usually remark “why don’t they just xyz?” came to mind. But I couldn’t think of a silver bullet or simple solution that I could do that would fix things and would keep us, who loved and still love each other, from breaking up. All those times when I thought that saying or believing you love each other was all you needed to make it. Suddenly all disproved. 
I got up to go. *“You’re leaving?”* She asked. She started hugging me, crying and saying she didn’t want me to go. I was crying too, head down and into her arms.  

I sat back down and tried again to get the answer I wanted, probably stumbling through my words. I wanted to get my point across without being blunt and being as delicate and nice as possible. Still in tears, she didn’t signal that she would change or become who I wanted her to be. I told her that I admired her, and that she was an inspiration to me. I admitted, breaking down again, that she had changed my life significantly for the better. I’m holding back tears as I write this part.  

We sat there for a bit teary eyed and in silence. I stopped crying. I picked up my bag again and stepped out of her room, and put my shoes on. She followed me outside. I didn’t have the heart to ask for my stuff back yet, even though that was why I brought the bag. I gave her one last hug.   

*“I’m sorry”* 

*“Why are you sorry?”*   

*“I didn’t want things to be this way”*  

*“Me too”* she nodded and said. I stepped outside and watched the door close, her face watching me in the gap till the door shut.     

I quickly walked away, and shrugged. Again, why the crying and unwillingness to concede? I wondered if she was looking through the peep hole.
I decided to walk home to think about what had just happened.     

“Thank you for showing me what it’s like to be loved” she texted me.   

I turned around and took a last look at her building.
